▾ What the app does for students

When a student opens the assignment link, the app walks them through the whole activity in the browser. Nothing they do leaves their device until they download and submit their files.

  1. Opens the reading from your link — no download or file upload.
  2. Microphone check, then reads the passage aloud while the app records their voice and times them. A clear "you are being recorded" notice shows, and they can pause and resume.
  3. Marks new vocabulary by tapping words (with dictionary lookup) and can turn them into flashcards.
  4. Comprehension check — answers your questions — then three short reflection questions.
  5. Downloads two files to submit: the audio recording and a PDF reading record (annotated reading, answers, time, words-per-minute, and any pauses).

How the levels are estimated About readability formulas

These formulas estimate difficulty from surface features — sentence length, word length, and syllable counts — not concept load or background-knowledge demands. A passage of short words and short sentences about an abstract idea will still score “easy.” Reading eight formulas together gives a sensible range rather than false precision. Lexile is proprietary and cannot be computed from an open formula, so it is not included. Dale–Chall and Spache are omitted because they depend on word-familiarity lists. Syllable counts use a standard heuristic and are approximate.
